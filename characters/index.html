Characters

  • Seron Tenebris

    The Main Hero of the Universe — The Dark Star. Born from the collapse of the First Timeline, Seron awakened with memories of a universe that no longer exists. He forged the Guardians to prevent the same cosmic downfall from repeating. His Dark Star core grants him limitless potential, but also ties him to the fate of every timeline. If Seron falls, the universe fractures.

  • Kole Rogue

    The Arsonist. Guardian. Seron's Clone. Created during a failed attempt to replicate Seron’s Dark Star essence, Kole emerged unstable but fiercely loyal. His Fire Axe is forged from Serlium embers — a weapon that burns through timelines. Though he shares Seron’s DNA, his personality is chaotic, unpredictable, and dangerously independent.

  • David Spike

    The Entropy. The Black Nebula. Once a cosmic wanderer drifting between dying universes, David was drawn to Seron’s reborn timeline. His power stems from entropy itself — collapse, decay, and rebirth. As the 2nd Leader of the Guardians, he balances Seron’s creation force with his own destructive nature. Together, they maintain cosmic equilibrium.

  • Connor Violet

    The Serlium Shield Hero. Guardian Commander. Connor was once a soldier in the Serlium Wars, where he mastered the art of defensive combat. His shield can withstand cosmic-level attacks, making him the frontline protector of the Guardians. As co‑leader beside David Spike, Connor ensures discipline, structure, and unity within the ranks.

  • Tom Letanio

    Guardian. Stand User of Indigo Fist. Tom was once an ordinary civilian until the Indigo Fist Stand awakened during the Angelic Purge — a catastrophic event that erased half the timeline. His Stand grants him time-stopping abilities and the Blue Phoenix transformation, allowing him to rise from near-death states. He is one of the few survivors of the Purge.

  • Andrew OverDark

    Time Breaker God. Guardian. Andrew was once a scholar of temporal physics until he discovered the forbidden OverDark equation — a formula that allowed him to break time itself. The Angelic Purge transformed him into a godlike being capable of rewriting causality. Despite his power, he chose to join Seron to prevent future timeline disasters.
